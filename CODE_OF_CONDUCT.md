# Code of Conduct

We want Archistry to be a friendly, useful place for architects, analysts,
consultants and developers of every experience level.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and accept it the same way.
- Welcome newcomers. A question that looks basic is a documentation bug.
- Credit other people's work.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or demeaning comments, personal or political
attacks, publishing someone's private information, sexual language or imagery,
and spam.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any space where
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@abhishakec](https://github.com/abhishakec)) may edit, hide or
remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
repeat offenders. To report a problem, open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/abhishakec/archistry/security/advisories/new)
with "Conduct" in the title, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Reports are handled privately.
